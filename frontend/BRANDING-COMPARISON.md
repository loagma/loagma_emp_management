## Branding Philosophy

### Simplification
We simplified verbose titles to make the UI cleaner:
- "Executive Dashboard" → "Dashboard"
- "Analytics Overview" → "Analytics"
- "Automation Center" → "Automation"

### Consistency
All branding now consistently uses "Loagma Employee Management" as the full name and "Loagma" as the short form.

### Professional Tone
The new branding maintains a professional, business-focused tone while being more concise and modern.
